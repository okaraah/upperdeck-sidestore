Privacy

UpperDeck is an independent One Piece TCG companion. Contact upperdecktcg@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Your account

We use your email address and a stable account ID to sign you in. Google sign-in provides basic account information such as your name and email. Your username can change; it is not your account ID.

What other players can see

Signed-in players can find your profile, username, tournament results and deck lists. Shared tournaments show their participants, pairings, reported results and standings. A typed opponent name does not link that person to an UpperDeck account.

What stays private

Your account details, personal round notes, opening hands and other private round annotations are available to your account. Personal data is cached separately for each account on your device. Unsent edits remain on that device until they can sync.

Services we use

Supabase hosts authentication, database records and uploaded profile images in the project’s Ireland region. Brevo delivers login-code emails. Google provides the optional Google sign-in flow. GitHub Pages hosts these information pages. Card images and card information may load from external card-data services.

If you use Chat, the messages you submit are sent through our server to OpenRouter and a model provider to generate a reply. Chat does not automatically receive your decks, notes, hands or tournament library. Avoid sending sensitive information in chat.

Simulator statistics

Published simulator data consists of aggregate statistics and deck lists from public simulator histories. It is separate from UpperDeck players’ tournament records. Published files do not include raw games, player identities, hands or bounties. Dataset coverage and period methodology are shown in the app’s Sim data information.

Deletion and recovery

You can delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and personal library and clears its local cache. Shared results needed by other participants remain with your player identity anonymized; another participant can take over hosting. Other players’ independently written notes and reports may still refer to encounters.

If you cannot access your account, email support from the address associated with it. Reports are accessible to the project’s maintainers for abuse review. We do not sell account data or use advertising trackers in the app.
